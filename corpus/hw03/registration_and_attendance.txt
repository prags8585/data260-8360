SJSU ID

Use your SJSUOne login information to sign into MySJSU. Your SJSU ID, also known as your Tower ID, is the number found on the front of your Tower Card. Your ID cannot be changed. New students receive this information from Admissions Office communications shortly after submitting a completed application for admission.

Primary Name (Legal Name)

Will be used on all official university documentation such as the diploma, official/unofficial transcript, the SJSU ID (Tower) Card, and any documentation involving financial aid or student accounts. Must be used when conducting official university business. Will be used for student's SJSU email address in this format: FirstName.LastName@sjsu.edu.

Preferred Name

San Jose State University recognizes that members of our community may choose names other than their legal names to identify themselves. SJSU allows preferred names as long as the name is not used to misrepresent or evade legal obligations. Inappropriate use of the preferred name policy may result in a violation of the Student Conduct Code and be referred to the Office of Student Conduct and Ethical Development.

Current students can set up a "preferred name" by changing first and/or middle names on MySJSU. The preferred name will only be seen on Class Rosters and Grade Rosters. The student's Primary name (Legal name) will appear on all official documents such as transcripts and diplomas.

Password

You can set your password for the first time or reset it if you have forgotten it by navigating to One.SJSU.edu and clicking on the Password Help tile. Remember, your password is cASe seNsiTIVE! If you are having difficulty resetting your password, please contact itservicedesk@sjsu.edu for assistance.

Appointments and Class Schedule
Do your research and plan your schedule

Check your enrollment appointment on your MySJSU account by logging on to One.SJSU.edu. You will not be able to register before your appointment time, but you can enter the system, run MyProgress and MyPlanner, and research course offerings, degree requirements, course prerequisites, etc. You will not be able to register until you clear registration holds. It is your responsibility to monitor your account to see when the appointment time is posted. Note that enrollment appointments are not mailed or emailed to students.

Create a registration worksheet before logging on

Build a sample schedule and have alternate schedules or classes prepared in the event courses are either closed or cancelled. SJSU offers course listings in 3 ways: MyScheduler to find schedule options; MySJSU class schedule search function with real-time updates of available courses; and the SJSU Catalog with instructions, policies, procedures and course listings, searchable and linked to degree templates.

Course Offerings and Cancelled Classes

The courses listed in the online SJSU Schedule of Classes reflect the scheduled offerings by the departments available at the publication deadline. The online schedule of classes posted at MySJSU provides real time status of classes. Departments have the option, based on enrollment, to cancel and/or add sections or courses.

Priority Groups

Current units-in-progress is used to determine class level for registration purposes. All priority groups are established per University Policy F17-4.

Group One - Priority Students: students required by external agencies such as the NCAA, or by law, to receive priority; students whose contributions to the university are recognized as being so extensive that their graduation would be postponed; students serving on Senate committees requiring student participation; students who are part of a group with a contractual agreement with SJSU to provide a full course load (AEC certified students, AEC note takers, Associated Students Board of Directors, Student Fairness Committee Members, NCAA Athletics, Guardian Scholars, Reciprocal Exchange students, Veterans).

Group Two: graduating seniors and remaining graduating students (bachelors- and graduate-level students who have a graduation application on file with an anticipated graduation date for the current or next semester).

Group Three: Non-graduating graduate students based on rotating alphabetical groupings.

Group Four: Seniors based on rotating alphabetical groupings.

Group Five: Second Baccalaureate students based on rotating alphabetical groupings.

Group Six: Juniors based on rotating alphabetical groupings.

Group Seven: Sophomore and continuing Freshmen based on rotating alphabetical groupings.

Note: First-Time Frosh registration is completed through Frosh Orientation. Incoming transfer students have a registration date dependent on when they matriculate, or their Transfer Orientation date.

Adding a Class

Navigate to One.SJSU.edu. Enter your SJSU ID and Password to log into MySJSU. Navigate to Academics > Add a Class. Select the correct term. Choose the class you wish to add.

Two ways to select a class: if you know the Class Number, type the 5 digit Class Number and click Enter; or click the green "Search Button" (make sure the Course Career is set to Graduate or Undergraduate) and click "Select Class" to place it in your shopping cart.

Once you have chosen a class, a "Class Enrollment Options" page will appear. Verify the course, enter permission number if required, select any related component classes, and click okay. The Add Classes page will appear showing your classes in your shopping cart. You must click "Finish Enrolling" to actually add the class.

Component Classes: if the course has related components attached (e.g., lab, seminar), the system will prompt you to select the one you wish to add.

Department or Instructor Consent: classes designated "Department Consent Required" require a Permission Number (add code). Most classes will require a Class Permission Number if you are adding during the Late Registration period, beginning on the first day of instruction.

Switching Class Sections: you must drop the section (lecture and labs/seminar, as well as related components or corequisites) before adding the new class section(s).

Warning - Finish Enrolling: once you have added all classes to your shopping cart, click "Proceed to Step 2 of 3," review your enrollment, then click "Finish Enrolling" to submit your registration.

Verify Add: check for any errors (e.g., prerequisites not met, class full), then check your class schedule to confirm all added classes appear.

"Instructor Consent" or "Department Consent": if a registration error indicates "Instructor Consent" is required, go to the instructor to obtain the Permission number. "Department Consent" requires going to the department that offers the course. Permission numbers become 'used' only after a successful enrollment request.

Adding Classes on the First Day of Instruction

At the first class meeting, Group 3 graduating students shall have top priority for any available spaces. An impartial procedure (such as a lottery or random selection) shall be used to choose, from among the graduating seniors, those who will fill available spaces. Automated waitlists run through the first nine days of the semester for most courses. Permission numbers will be required to add courses after that date (University Policy F20-1).

Adding Classes after the Last Day to Add: before the Census date, students can submit the Pre-census Late Enrollment petition form to the Registrar's office (valid through census date per University Policy F09-2). After census date, students can petition for a late add using the Post-Census Late Enrollment form.

Late Fee Assessed

Students registering for classes after census are assessed a late registration fee of $200.00, which begins after census for any classes added (includes Retroactive Adds, which begin the first day after instruction ends). Students in the Step to College programs are not required to pay the late registration fee.

Waitlisting

SJSU has "waitlisting" for all classes. Waitlisting puts you in line to get a space when a class is full (closed class), should a space become available. See University Policy F20-1 for more details.

The waitlist process runs nightly from the first day of Advance Registration through 9 days after the start of the term. You may sign up for the waitlist via MySJSU: at "Step 1, Select classes to add," search for your preferred course; if the class is full and the waitlist option is available, you will see a yellow waitlist icon; click the checkbox next to "Waitlist if class is full"; complete the remaining steps; you will see a message indicating you are on the waitlist and your current position number.

After the third week of Advance Registration, the automated waitlist process adds students based on priority, and a Variable Waitlist Unit Limit applies (defines the available units a student can waitlist at a given time). There is no guarantee you will be enrolled in the class from the waitlist.

Registration Procedures: you may place yourself on the waitlist if a class is already full. If you are already enrolled in another section of the same course, you will not be moved from the waitlist unless you have set up the switch as a "swap." When you place yourself on a waitlist, you hold a position that changes as students ahead of you get enrolled or drop off the waitlist. If enrolled from waitlist, it is your responsibility to drop the class if you decide not to attend; failure to properly drop will result in a "WU" grade which will lower your GPA.

Registration issues that may prevent you from moving from waitlist to enrollment (check before you put yourself on waitlist): a hold on your account; not meeting prerequisites or corequisites; exceeding your Enrollment Limit; a time conflict with an already-enrolled class; already being enrolled in another section of the same course; repeating a class (will not be able to enroll until a later date); or a course with components requiring space to open in all sections. If any of these apply, you will be skipped over and the next student on the waitlist will be considered.

Dropping a Class

Go to MySJSU, enter your SJSU ID and Password. If you drop a class, you must do so prior to the time you submit payment to ensure registration fees are properly assessed. Navigate to "Self Service" > Student center > "Drop a class", click "Drop Classes", check the box next to each class you wish to drop, click "Drop Selected Classes", review your selection and click "Finish Dropping." It is the student's responsibility to make sure classes are dropped.

Dropping Without Penalty: courses may be dropped without academic penalty during the first nine days of classes. Courses dropped during this initial period will not appear on the student's transcript. With instructor consent, students may late add a class up to the add deadline.

Dropping Classes After Drop Deadline: students may petition to late or retroactively drop a course or withdraw from an entire semester of courses for serious and compelling reasons after the drop deadline. Conditions are defined as "serious and compelling" only if beyond the student's control and must have occurred after the drop deadline. A personal statement with supporting documentation must be submitted with the late and retroactive drop and withdrawal forms. Submission of these forms, even with an instructor's signature, does not constitute approval; students must continue attending class and complete all assignments until the petition is approved or denied.

Instructor Drops

Instructors are permitted to drop a student who fails to establish a committed presence in the class. "Establishing a committed presence" is defined in University Policy F15-3 as: for in-person classes, attending the first class meeting or informing the instructor of intention to continue before 48 hours after the first official class meeting; for online classes, logging on to the LMS (Canvas) class shell on the first scheduled day or informing the instructor before 48 hours after the official class start date. Instructors may (but are not required to) drop students who do not meet stated course prerequisites. It is the student's responsibility to make sure classes are dropped.

Late Drop Petitions

Students may petition to Late Drop for serious and compelling reasons after the drop deadline. Petitions are available at Undergraduate Education for undergraduates and GAPE for graduate students.

Academic Renewal

Graduating seniors with a GPA below 2.0 in the major, minor, SJSU, or overall college coursework, may request that all grades earned in a previous semester (or two semesters) be disregarded, to permit graduation. If approved, all grades and units earned during the semester will be disregarded when calculating GPAs for graduation, but the courses and grades will still appear on the transcript.

All of the following conditions must be met: applied for graduation and ready to graduate; GPA below 2.0 in a category required for graduation; at least 5 years elapsed since the semester to be disregarded; at least a 3.0 GPA in the most recent 15 units at SJSU, or 2.5 in the past 30 units, or 2.0 in the past 45 units; no courses or units earned during the disregarded semester may count toward graduation; documented extenuating circumstances showing performance is not representative of ability.

Cancellation of Registration or Withdrawal From the Institution

Students who find it necessary to cancel their registration or withdraw from all classes after enrolling are required to follow the university's official withdrawal procedures. Failure to follow formal procedures may result in an obligation to pay fees as well as failing grades in all courses and the need to apply for readmission. Students who receive financial aid must consult with Financial Aid prior to withdrawing regarding any required return or repayment of grant or loan assistance.

Restrictions

Graduate students cannot enroll in lower division classes during Advance Registration (lower division classes start on the first day of instruction). Matriculated SJSU students cannot concurrently enroll in Open University.

Students returning after an absence of more than one semester (without a formal leave of absence petition) must reapply for admission before access to registration is granted.

Maximum Unit Load: during Advance Registration a cap is placed on the maximum number of units students can enroll in; after a specified date the cap is raised. Students wishing to enroll in excess units must submit an Excess Units petition to their Department Chair.

Repeating a Class: any matriculated student repeating a course for the first time shall be allowed to register for the course during Advance Registration no earlier than approximately three weeks prior to the start of the semester (University Policy F08-2). Students who received a "W" are treated as first-time registrants. Students repeating a class for two or more times must submit a completed petition to the Registrar's Office.

Returning After an Absence: if in good standing and not disenrolled for non-payment of fees, stopping out for one semester does not require reapplying for admission, but you must enroll the semester immediately following. An absence of a full year (not enrolling in the next spring and fall terms) requires reapplying for admission via Cal State Apply.

First Year Course Placement - English and Mathematics/Quantitative Reasoning Supported Instruction

Students required to participate in Supported Instruction in Math and/or English may be restricted from enrolling in certain classes for which they do not meet prerequisites, and are required to enroll in an appropriate Math and/or English class during their first year and each successive term until completed with a grade of at least C-. All entering frosh assigned to enrollment category W-II or W-III must enroll in their A2 course(s) during their first academic year; those assigned M-II or M-III must enroll in B4 course(s) during their first academic year.

Entering frosh who have not completed A2 or B4 within a year of first registration will be placed on Administrative Academic Probation; within two years, they will be administratively academically disqualified.

Writing Skills Test (WST) - Registration Restriction

You must satisfy the WST requirement in order to register for any SJSU Studies (Area R, S, V, and Z) course, even if taken for a major requirement instead of GE. SJSU offers courses (LLD 100A and ENGL 100A) that satisfy the WST requirement if completed with a grade of "C" or better.

CSU Interim COVID-19 Vaccination Policy

Entering CSU students are required to present proof of specified immunizations before the beginning of their first term of enrollment. All individuals who access Campus/Programs must be Fully Vaccinated against COVID-19, subject to Medical Exemption (verified by a certified or licensed healthcare professional) or Religious Exemption (sincerely held religious belief, observance, or practice).

CSU Immunization Requirements

Measles, Mumps, and Rubella (MMR): Two doses with first dose on or after 1st birthday, or positive titer. Hepatitis B: new students 18 or younger at start of first term must provide proof of full immunization (three timed doses over 4-6 months). Varicella (Chickenpox): two doses or positive titer. Tetanus, Diphtheria, Pertussis (Tdap): one dose after age 7. Meningococcal conjugate: one dose on or after age 16, for students 21 or younger. Tuberculosis Screening/Risk Assessment: all incoming students must complete a TB risk questionnaire; higher-risk students should undergo skin or blood testing within one year of CSU entry.

Immigration Requirements for Licensure

Students who will require a professional or commercial license from a government agency to engage in an occupation for which the CSU may be training them must meet the immigration requirements of the Personal Responsibility and Work Opportunity Reconciliation Act (P.L. 104-193) to achieve licensure.

Registration Holds

Holds on student accounts and records will prevent students from registering. Monetary holds of $50.00 or more will block registration, diplomas and other miscellaneous services.

College of Engineering Advising: all College of Engineering undergraduates must see an advisor every semester prior to registration.

College of Science Advising: all undergraduate College of Science majors and graduate students in Chemistry, Computer Science and Physics must see a major advisor prior to registering every semester.

UASC Advising Hold: all Undeclared and Undeclared-PreNursing students are required to see an advisor in Undergraduate Advising and Success Center (UASC) prior to registering every semester.

Financial Holds: a balance due on your student account will block registration until the debt has been paid; payments are applied to the oldest balance first.

Online Title IX Training Hold: all incoming students are expected to complete online Title IX training addressing sexual assault prevention; a hold (HW1) will be placed if not completed.

AlcoholEdu Hold: all incoming undergraduates are expected to complete AlcoholEdu, an online training course addressing alcohol abuse prevention; a hold (HW2) will be placed if not completed.

Hepatitis B Immunization Series: first-time SJSU students who will be 18 years or younger on the first day of instruction may be required to submit proof of full immunization or immunity against Hepatitis B.

International Student Health Insurance: proof of health insurance is required prior to registration each semester.

Measles and Rubella: new or readmitted students born on or after January 1, 1957 may be required to submit proof of full immunization or immunity against Measles and Rubella.

Missing Document Holds: students who owe documents or transcripts to Undergraduate Admissions must submit them by the deadline posted in MySJSU before being allowed to register.

Probation Holds: undergraduates with SJSU cumulative GPA below 2.00 (3.00 for graduate students), or reinstated on probation status, must see their major advisor or a UASC advisor before registering, each term until no longer on probation.

Graduation Registration Restriction: students who have not taken the WST by the time they earn 75 units (counting no more than 70 transfer units) may have their registration held. Students with 120 or more units who have not applied for graduation will not be allowed to register until they submit that application. Students with 150 or more units may be required to meet with a UASC advisor before registration.
