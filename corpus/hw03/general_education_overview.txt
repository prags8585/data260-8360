General Education Requirements Overview - San José State University
Source: https://catalog.sjsu.edu/content.php?catoid=10&navoid=661

General Education Requirements Overview

Core General Education
Fundamental Skills of an Educated Person

Oral Communication (A1): 3 units
Written Communication I (A2): 3 units
Critical Thinking and Writing (A3): 3 units
Mathematics/Quantitative Reasoning (B4): 3 units
Subtotal: 12 units

Fundamental Knowledge of an Educated Person

Physical Science (B1): 3 units
Life Science (B2): 3 units
One lab course in either Physical or Life Science (B3)
Arts (C1): 3 units
Humanities (C2): 3 units
A third course from either Arts or Humanities (C1 or C2): 3 units
Social Sciences (D): 6-9 units
Human Understanding & Development (E): 3 units
Ethnic Studies (F): 0-3 units
Subtotal: 27 units

SJSU Studies - Upper Division General Education (R, S, and V)
Integrated Knowledge of an Educated Person

Earth, Environment, and Sustainability (R): 3 units
Self, Society & Equality in the U.S. (S): 3 units
Cultures and Global Understanding (V): 3 units
Subtotal: 9 units

Graduation Requirements

American Institutions (may be satisfied in Core): 0-6 units
Physical Education (two units or courses of activity are required): 0-2 units
Writing in the Disciplines (WID): 0-3 units
Subtotal: 0-11 units

Total General Education Units

Core: 39 units
Upper Division: 9 units
Subtotal: 48 units
