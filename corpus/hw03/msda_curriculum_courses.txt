What You’ll Learn
The Master’s in Applied Data Intelligence curriculum integrates rigorous course work
with real-world case studies and collaborative projects alongside Silicon Valley companies,
ensuring you graduate with industry-ready skills.
In just 30 credits, you undergo advanced training to design and deliver real-world
data solutions. You learn how to process and analyze data from multiple sources, build
scalable data pipelines, extract meaningful insights and develop full-stack systems.
By combining data engineering, applied analytics and AI techniques, you gain the ability
to solve complex problems from initial data collection through final deployment.
Beyond core courses, you can specialize in data engineering. This track builds expertise
in data warehousing, big data and distributed systems while advancing your skills
in generative AI and prompt engineering for scalable, real-world applications.

Culminating Experience
To solidify your theoretical knowledge and applied data expertise, your time in the
Master of Science in Applied Data Intelligence program concludes with a culminating
experience.
Your first option is to complete a thesis, which includes a two-course sequence. You
will conduct original research under the guidance of a faculty committee and produce
a thesis that meets San José State University’s graduate education standards.
The second option is to complete a project. In this two-course applied sequence, you
collaborate with a small team and a faculty advisor to design and execute a comprehensive
data or development project, culminating in a detailed written report and presentation.
Whichever path you choose, you prepare for advanced professional roles by integrating
data engineering, data intelligence and AI technologies concepts into real-world problem
solving.

What is Applied Data Intelligence?
Applied Data Intelligence uses data, analytics and emerging technologies like artificial
intelligence to solve real-world problems and inform decision-making. It goes beyond
collecting data and discerning patterns to interpreting insights, building predictive
models and applying findings to improve business strategy, operations and outcomes.
This interdisciplinary field blends technical skills with critical thinking to turn
complex information into actionable solutions.
