Academic Standing, Grades, and Registration

The Registrar's Office Calendar includes dates and deadlines for registration, grades, and academic standing for Fall 2022 and Spring 2023.

Exams

The Testing Office site has information about the Writing Skills Test (WST) and Test of Essential Academic Skills (TEAS). The Final Examination Schedule includes dates for final exams, including a make-up day for fall and spring terms. The final exam for Winter and Summer sessions are normally scheduled for the last day of the term.

Fees and Housing

Payment due dates are based on when you register (see Bursar's payment schedule). The Office of Financial Aid and Scholarships posts deadlines regarding financial aid and scholarships. University Housing Services includes dates for housing applications.

New Students

New students need to attend a New Student Orientation (dates vary by Freshman, Graduate, or Transfer status).
